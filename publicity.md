# Publicity

## Key message

Campus Connect is a relaxed, welcoming event where students can meet new people, discover the campus and join activities together. No experience or sign-up fee needed.

## Publicity channels

- Posters on student notice boards near the library, canteen and lecture halls.
- Short announcement slides shown before lectures, with lecturers' permission.
- Posts on the student council's social media pages.
- An event listing in the weekly student newsletter.
- Word of mouth through club and CCA leaders.

## Timeline

| When | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 3 weeks before | Finalise key message and poster design. |
| 2 weeks before | Put up posters and publish the first social media post. |
| 1 week before | Newsletter listing and lecture announcements. |
| 2 days before | Reminder post with programme highlights. |
| Event day | Live updates and photos of the venue (no close-up photos of individuals without consent). |

## Registration

- Students register through a simple online form linked from every poster and post.
- The form collects only what is needed, such as first name and course.
